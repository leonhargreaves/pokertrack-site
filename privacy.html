Policy

Last updated: June 2026

♣  PokerTrack does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Ever.

Overview

PokerTrack is a personal poker session tracker built for iPhone. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

The short version: all data you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect your information.

Data We Collect

We collect nothing. PokerTrack does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, usage data, or analytics.

Data Stored on Your Device

Session records you enter (game type, stakes, buy-in, cash-out, notes, and dates) are saved locally on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device unless you back it up through Apple's iCloud Backup service, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Third Parties

PokerTrack contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporters, or analytics tools. No data is shared with any third party under any circumstances.

Cookies & Tracking

The app does not use cookies, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting, or any other tracking technology.

Children's Privacy

PokerTrack does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Since we collect no data, any changes are unlikely to affect your privacy in practice.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at support@example.com.
