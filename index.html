Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a new session?
Tap the + button on the main screen to start a new session. Enter the game type, stakes, buy-in, and cash-out amount. PokerTrack automatically calculates your profit or loss and session duration.
Can I edit or delete a session after saving it?
Yes. Tap any session in your history to open it, then tap Edit to make changes or Delete to remove it. Deleted sessions cannot be recovered, so double-check before confirming.
Where is my data stored?
All your session data is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent to any server or third party. If you delete the app, your data will be lost — see the backup question below.
How do I back up my data?
Make sure iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup). PokerTrack's data will be included in your device backup automatically.
How are my stats calculated?
PokerTrack calculates your total profit/loss, hourly rate (total profit ÷ total hours played), return on investment, biggest winning and losing sessions, and running totals across your full history.
Does PokerTrack work offline?
Yes, entirely. PokerTrack does not require an internet connection at any point. Log sessions anywhere — at the table, on the tube, wherever.
Is PokerTrack free?
Yes, PokerTrack is completely free to download and use. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.
